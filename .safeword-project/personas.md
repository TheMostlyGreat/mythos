# Personas

Project-wide persona definitions. Every spec, JTBD, and scenario references
these by name or short code. Mythos is a **creative tool**, not a content
platform — so the personas center on the person making a story, not on an
audience consuming a catalog of others' stories.

## Author

**Role:** A person with a story in them who wants to bring it into the world, without being a trained novelist.

**Context:** Not a professional writer. Has taste, ideas, and emotional intent, but lacks craft, time, or the discipline to face a blank page. Wants to _make the meaningful decisions_ and have the drudgery (blank-page paralysis, continuity bookkeeping, prose mechanics) removed. The entertainment is the co-creation journey; the payoff is an output they feel deep ownership over. This is the **primary** persona — everything optimizes for them first. The Author also _reads_ their finished work in a lean-back mode (forks dialed down), so "Reader" is a mode of the Author, not yet a separate persona.

## Integrator

**Role:** A developer who invokes Mythos's generation capabilities inside their own product or workflow via the MCP server.

**Context:** Technical. Doesn't want the conversational creation UI — wants programmatic access to the engine (generate a story, a chapter, a character, a bible) from Claude, another assistant, or custom tooling. E.g., a game-master generating lore, an app embedding story generation. **Secondary** persona.
