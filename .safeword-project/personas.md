# Personas

## Writer

**Role:** A typical end-user with no formal writing background who wants to turn an idea they care about into a finished story.

**Context:** Not a trained author — someone who has a story in them but lacks the craft, time, or confidence to write it alone. Comes in with a rough premise and shapes it through conversation, then steers the draft by choosing at story forks, redirecting in plain language, or editing any part directly. Wants the drudgery _and_ the craft barrier removed — blank-page paralysis, continuity bookkeeping, "what comes next," formatting — while the meaningful creative decisions stay theirs, because those decisions are what make the result feel like their own. Works in short sessions and expects to pick up exactly where they left off. Also reads their finished story in a lean-back mode (the same engine with forks dialed down).

**Only persona.** The Writer is the single persona Mythos builds for. Two further roles appear in the forward-looking jobs but are explicitly **not personas** we serve today: an **Integrator** (a developer driving the engine programmatically over MCP) and a **Reader** of other people's stories (relevant only if Mythos ever becomes a content platform). Neither is built for until Mythos earns its way there.
