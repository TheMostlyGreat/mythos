# Romance Craft Framework

## Core Reader Experience Goal

**Make readers feel deeply emotionally invested in the romantic relationship and experience genuine satisfaction when the couple gets together**

---

## Essential Romance Craft Questions

### **Chemistry & Attraction Building**

- **How will you make readers feel the spark between your characters?** [First meeting electricity, sexual tension, emotional connection]
- **What specific moments will show undeniable chemistry?** [Dialogue that crackles, physical awareness, emotional vulnerability]
- **How will you balance instant attraction vs. slow-burn development?** [Love at first sight vs. enemies-to-lovers vs. friends-to-lovers]
- **What physical details will create sexual/romantic tension?** [Meaningful touches, near-misses, charged glances]

### **Emotional Beat Architecture**

- **What will be your relationship's emotional turning points?** [First kiss, first fight, breakup, reconciliation, confession]
- **How will you make readers ache for the characters to be together?** [Separation anxiety, obstacles that feel meaningful]
- **Which scenes will make readers swoon or get butterflies?** [Peak romantic moments, declarations, grand gestures]
- **How will you handle the emotional climax and resolution?** [Confession scene, final obstacle overcome, happily ever after]

### **Conflict & Obstacle Design**

- **What believable obstacles will keep your couple apart?** [Internal fears, external circumstances, misunderstandings, competing goals]
- **How will you avoid frustrating readers with artificial drama?** [Meaningful conflict vs. manufactured drama]
- **What stakes make the relationship feel important enough to fight for?** [What they each risk losing, what they gain together]
- **How will resolving conflicts strengthen the relationship?** [Growth through adversity, deeper understanding]

### **Character Arc Integration**

- **How will falling in love change each character for the better?** [Personal growth through relationship, healing old wounds]
- **What does each character need that only the other can provide?** [Complementary strengths, emotional healing, life completion]
- **How will their individual journeys interweave with the romantic arc?** [Career goals, family issues, personal fears addressed through love]
- **What will make readers believe this couple belongs together forever?** [Deep compatibility, mutual growth, shared values]

### **Fantasy Fulfillment**

- **What romantic fantasies will you deliver for your target audience?** [Alpha protector, best friend's brother, second chance, billionaire, etc.]
- **How will you make familiar tropes feel fresh and exciting?** [Unique twists, contemporary settings, diverse characters]
- **What wish fulfillment will readers get from your romance?** [Being cherished, passionate love, perfect partner, happy ending]
- **How will you balance fantasy with realistic relationship dynamics?** [Aspirational but believable, healthy relationship models]

### **Sensuality & Intimacy**

- **How will you build sexual tension throughout the story?** [Physical awareness, near-misses, charged moments, anticipation]
- **What level of explicit content serves your story and audience?** [Sweet/closed door, steamy, explicit - matching reader expectations]
- **How will you make intimate scenes emotionally meaningful?** [Connection to character growth, relationship milestones, emotional vulnerability]
- **What will make the physical relationship feel inevitable and right?** [Emotional intimacy leading to physical, perfect timing, mutual desire]

## Romance Subgenre Considerations

### **Contemporary Romance**

- **How will modern dating culture affect your story?** [Apps, social media, career pressures, lifestyle compatibility]
- **What current social issues will add depth?** [Work-life balance, mental health, diversity, economic pressures]

### **Historical Romance**

- **How will period constraints create meaningful conflict?** [Social class, gender roles, family expectations, societal rules]
- **What historical details will enhance the romantic fantasy?** [Period clothing, settings, customs that heighten romance]

### **Paranormal Romance**

- **How will supernatural elements enhance the romantic connection?** [Fated mates, magical bonds, otherworldly passion]
- **What unique obstacles does the paranormal world create?** [Species differences, immortality issues, magical threats]

### **Romantic Suspense**

- **How will external danger bring the couple closer together?** [Forced proximity, protection scenarios, trust building under pressure]
- **What will make the romance feel urgent and intense?** [Life-or-death stakes, time pressure, emotional vulnerability in crisis]

## Reader Satisfaction Checklist

### **Emotional Journey**

- [ ] **Anticipation**: Do readers eagerly anticipate the couple getting together?
- [ ] **Investment**: Do readers care deeply about both characters' happiness?
- [ ] **Satisfaction**: Does the resolution feel earned and fulfilling?
- [ ] **Replay Value**: Will readers want to reread favorite romantic scenes?

### **Fantasy Fulfillment**

- [ ] **Wish Fulfillment**: Does the romance deliver what readers came for?
- [ ] **Escapism**: Does the story provide satisfying emotional escape?
- [ ] **Aspiration**: Do readers wish they had this kind of love?
- [ ] **Comfort**: Does the ending provide emotional security and happiness?

### **Character Connection**

- [ ] **Relatability**: Can readers see themselves in at least one character?
- [ ] **Chemistry**: Do readers feel the spark between characters?
- [ ] **Growth**: Do both characters become better people through love?
- [ ] **Believability**: Does the relationship feel real and lasting?

## Romance Craft Techniques

### **Dialogue That Builds Romance**

- **Banter and Wit**: Playful exchanges that show intellectual compatibility
- **Emotional Vulnerability**: Characters sharing fears, dreams, past hurts
- **Subtext**: What characters don't say but readers understand
- **Unique Voice**: Each character's distinct way of speaking and thinking

### **Physical Tension Techniques**

- **Meaningful Touch**: Casual contact that carries emotional weight
- **Near Misses**: Almost-kisses and interrupted moments
- **Body Language**: Physical awareness and unconscious attraction cues
- **Sensory Details**: Scent, warmth, texture that create intimacy

### **Pacing Romance**

- **Slow Burn**: Gradual building of attraction and emotional intimacy
- **Fast Burn**: Immediate attraction with emotional depth building
- **Push-Pull**: Advance and retreat cycles that build tension
- **Climax Timing**: When to deliver key romantic moments for maximum impact

---

_Remember: Romance readers come for the emotional experience. Every scene should either build romantic tension, develop character feelings, or advance the relationship arc. The goal is to make readers fall in love alongside your characters._
