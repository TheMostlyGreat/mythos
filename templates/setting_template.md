## DETAILED COMPONENTS
*The AI will determine which of these need detailed development based on your story:*

**Possible Deep Dive Components:**
- **Geography** (landscape, climate, natural features, physical layout)
- **Locations** (specific places, buildings, landmarks, neighborhoods)  
- **Culture** (customs, traditions, social norms, religion, beliefs)
- **Politics** (government systems, laws, political tensions, factions)
- **Economy** (trade, currency, class systems, industries, wealth)
- **Technology** (tech level, magic systems, innovations, tools)
- **Daily Life** (occupations, clothing, food, entertainment, routines)
- **History** (past events, conflicts, founding stories, legends)

*Components selected for deep dive will be developed in separate detailed files.*

## RESEARCH GUIDANCE

**For Contemporary Settings:**
- Research real locations, cultures, and social systems
- Verify current events, technology, and cultural practices
- Ensure authentic representation of real places and peoples

**For Historical Settings:**
- Research historical accuracy for time period
- Verify social structures, technology, and cultural practices
- Balance historical authenticity with story needs

**For Fantasy/Sci-Fi Settings:**
- Ground speculative elements in logical systems
- Research real-world inspirations for authenticity
- Ensure internal consistency for magic/tech systems
