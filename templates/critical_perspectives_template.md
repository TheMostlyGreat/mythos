## Example: Critical Perspectives for Fantasy Adventure

**Primary Frameworks:**
1. **Ecocriticism**
   - Key Concepts: Nature as character, environmental themes
   - Story Application: Magic tied to natural world, industrialization as threat
   - Character Analysis: Heroes connected to nature vs. villains exploiting it
   - Relevant Theorists: Lawrence Buell, Cheryll Glotfelty

2. **Mythological Criticism**
   - Key Concepts: Archetypal patterns, hero's journey
   - Story Application: Classic quest structure with modern twists
   - Character Analysis: Archetypal roles (mentor, shadow, ally)
   - Relevant Theorists: Joseph Campbell, Northrop Frye

3. **Postcolonial Criticism**
   - Key Concepts: Power dynamics, cultural representation
   - Story Application: Different cultures and their interactions
   - Character Analysis: How characters navigate cultural differences
   - Relevant Theorists: Edward Said, Homi Bhabha
