# Science Fiction Craft Framework

## Core Reader Experience Goal
**Transport readers to believable future/alternate worlds, make them question current reality through speculation, and provide sense of wonder about scientific possibilities**

---

## Essential Speculation Craft Questions

### **Scientific Foundation & Speculation**
- **What scientific concepts or technologies drive your story's core premise?** [Hard science, theoretical physics, biology, technology, space travel, AI, genetics]
- **How will you balance scientific accuracy with narrative excitement?** [Research depth, plausible extrapolation, dramatic license, accessible explanation]
- **What "what if" questions will drive your technological speculation?** [Consequences of innovation, unintended effects, social implications, evolutionary pressures]
- **How will you make complex scientific concepts accessible without dumbing down?** [Character expertise, gradual revelation, analogies, learning through experience]

### **World-Building & Future Society**
- **How will technological advancement change human society and relationships?** [Social structures, communication, work, family, identity, power dynamics]
- **What will make your future world feel lived-in and authentic?** [Cultural evolution, economic systems, everyday technology, social norms, generational differences]
- **How will you show rather than tell technological integration?** [Characters using tech naturally, cultural assumptions, background details, conflict arising from tech]
- **What aspects of current humanity will remain constant despite technological change?** [Core emotions, relationship dynamics, moral struggles, basic needs]

### **Sense of Wonder & Ideas**
- **What will make readers feel awe about scientific possibilities?** [Scale of concepts, implications for humanity, beauty of discovery, intellectual excitement]
- **How will you present mind-expanding concepts that challenge reader assumptions?** [New ways of thinking, alternative perspectives, paradigm shifts, consciousness expansion]
- **What discoveries or revelations will fundamentally change how characters view reality?** [Scientific breakthroughs, alien contact, consciousness insights, universe mechanics]
- **How will you balance intellectual concepts with emotional resonance?** [Human impact of ideas, personal stakes in discovery, emotional cost of knowledge]

### **Character & Technology Integration**
- **How do your characters relate to and use advanced technology?** [Natural integration, dependency issues, enhancement vs. replacement, identity questions]
- **What will make your characters feel human despite futuristic circumstances?** [Recognizable emotions, relatable goals, familiar struggles in unfamiliar contexts]
- **How will technology create or solve your characters' central conflicts?** [Enhancement vs. limitation, intended vs. actual use, social consequences, personal costs]
- **What will make readers care about characters in extraordinary circumstances?** [Universal emotions, relatable motivations, personal growth, moral choices]

### **Social & Philosophical Implications**
- **What contemporary issues will your future world illuminate or extrapolate?** [Current trends extended, warning scenarios, optimistic projections, alternative solutions]
- **How will you explore the human condition through speculative elements?** [What makes us human, consciousness questions, evolution of society, ethical dilemmas]
- **What moral or philosophical questions will your technology raise?** [AI rights, genetic modification, privacy, equality, environmental impact, human enhancement]
- **How will you make philosophical concepts concrete and dramatic?** [Character decisions, plot consequences, world-building details, conflict generation]

## Science Fiction Subgenre Considerations

### **Hard Science Fiction**
- **How will you maintain scientific rigor while creating compelling drama?** [Accurate extrapolation, realistic consequences, technical detail balance, expert consultation]
- **What will make complex scientific concepts exciting rather than overwhelming?** [Character discovery, practical applications, wonder maintenance, accessible explanation]

### **Space Opera**
- **How will you create epic scope while maintaining character intimacy?** [Personal stakes in galactic events, individual agency in vast conflicts, relationship focus]
- **What will make alien cultures feel truly alien yet comprehensible?** [Different logic systems, alternative social structures, unique communication methods]

### **Cyberpunk/Tech Noir**
- **How will you explore the dark side of technological advancement?** [Corporate control, identity loss, reality distortion, social inequality, human obsolescence]
- **What will make high-tech/low-life contrast feel meaningful?** [Economic disparity, access inequality, technology as oppression tool, human resistance]

### **Post-Apocalyptic**
- **How will you show human resilience and adaptation in extreme circumstances?** [Community formation, survival innovation, hope maintenance, humanity preservation]
- **What will make your apocalypse feel unique and your recovery believable?** [Specific causes, realistic consequences, logical adaptation, meaningful rebuilding]

### **Time Travel**
- **How will you handle paradoxes and timeline logic consistently?** [Clear rules, consistent application, logical consequences, plot service]
- **What will make time travel feel like more than a plot device?** [Character growth through experience, thematic resonance, emotional weight, philosophical exploration]

### **Dystopian Fiction**
- **How will you critique current society without being preachy?** [Extrapolation of trends, character-driven resistance, showing rather than telling, multiple perspectives]
- **What will make your dystopia feel both alien and uncomfortably familiar?** [Current issues magnified, recognizable progression, logical development, personal impact]

## Reader Engagement Checklist

### **Intellectual Satisfaction**
- [ ] **Wonder**: Do readers feel awe at scientific/technological possibilities?
- [ ] **Logic**: Are speculative elements internally consistent and believable?
- [ ] **Learning**: Do readers gain new perspectives or knowledge?
- [ ] **Relevance**: Do future scenarios illuminate current issues?

### **Emotional Investment**
- [ ] **Character Connection**: Do readers care about characters despite unusual circumstances?
- [ ] **Stakes**: Are consequences of scientific/technological elements personally meaningful?
- [ ] **Growth**: Do characters develop through their speculative experiences?
- [ ] **Resolution**: Do endings satisfy both intellectually and emotionally?

### **World Immersion**
- [ ] **Believability**: Does the world feel like a natural evolution from current reality?
- [ ] **Authenticity**: Do technological and social elements feel lived-in?
- [ ] **Consistency**: Are world-building rules maintained throughout?
- [ ] **Exploration**: Do readers want to spend more time in this world?

## Advanced Science Fiction Techniques

### **Exposition & Information Management**
- **Show Don't Tell**: Revealing world details through character actions and dialogue
- **Iceberg Theory**: Implying larger world through specific details
- **Gradual Revelation**: Building understanding through progressive disclosure
- **Character Knowledge**: Using POV character limitations to control information flow

### **Scientific Speculation Methods**
- **Extrapolation**: Taking current trends to logical conclusions
- **Combination**: Merging existing technologies in new ways
- **Inversion**: Exploring opposite outcomes from expected results
- **Consequence Mapping**: Following technological changes through society

### **World-Building Integration**
- **Seamless Technology**: Making advanced tech feel natural and integrated
- **Cultural Evolution**: Showing how technology changes social norms and relationships
- **Economic Implications**: Understanding how technology affects work and economics
- **Generational Differences**: Varying comfort levels with technological change

### **Character in Speculative Context**
- **Technology Relationship**: How characters interact with and perceive advanced tech
- **Identity Questions**: How technological change affects sense of self
- **Moral Adaptation**: Characters navigating new ethical landscapes
- **Human Constancy**: Maintaining recognizable humanity in changed circumstances

---

## Science Fiction Craft Techniques

### **Opening World Establishment**
- **In Media Res**: Starting in the middle of futuristic action
- **Familiar Anchors**: Using recognizable elements to ease readers into speculation
- **Immediate Wonder**: Early demonstration of speculative elements
- **Character Normalcy**: Showing how extraordinary elements are ordinary to characters

### **Scientific Concept Introduction**
- **Character Expertise**: Using knowledgeable characters to explain naturally
- **Learning Curve**: Protagonist discovery mirrors reader understanding
- **Practical Application**: Showing concepts through use rather than explanation
- **Analogy**: Comparing unfamiliar concepts to familiar experiences

### **Tension & Conflict Creation**
- **Technology Failure**: When advanced systems break down or malfunction
- **Unintended Consequences**: Scientific advancement creating unexpected problems
- **Social Resistance**: Characters fighting against technological change
- **Ethical Dilemmas**: Moral choices created by new capabilities

### **Resolution & Implications**
- **Consequence Exploration**: Following through on speculative premise implications
- **Character Growth**: How characters change through speculative experiences
- **Social Change**: How conflicts reshape world or society
- **Future Implications**: Suggesting continued evolution beyond story end

---

*Remember: Science fiction readers seek both intellectual stimulation and emotional engagement. They want to explore possibilities while caring about characters. The goal is to use speculation to illuminate both future possibilities and current human nature.*
