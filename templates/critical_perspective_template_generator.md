## Content Guidelines for LLM Generation

### **Core Analytical Purpose**

- Start with action verb (Reveal, Expose, Illuminate, Examine, etc.)
- Focus on what insights this perspective provides
- Connect to both analytical understanding and practical storytelling
- Examples: "Reveal how power structures shape character relationships" or "Examine how formal elements create meaning"

### **Essential Questions Format**

- Mix of "How will you..." and "What will..." formats
- Focus on practical application to storytelling, not just analysis
- Include 3-6 specific examples in brackets after each question
- Questions should help writers apply the critical lens while creating
- Balance analytical insight with storytelling craft

### **Focus Areas to Include (choose 4-5)**

- Primary concern of the critical perspective
- Character/identity/psychology elements
- Social/cultural/political dynamics
- Formal/structural/aesthetic considerations
- Reader consciousness/awareness impact

### **Genre Applications**

- Choose 4-5 major genres
- Show how the critical perspective applies differently across genres
- Each gets exactly 2 questions focusing on genre-specific applications
- Address both popular and literary applications

### **Analysis Categories (choose 4)**

- Core perspective focus (main analytical concern)
- Character/social dynamics (relationships and identity)
- Narrative/structural elements (form and technique)
- Reader impact/consciousness (awareness and transformation)

### **Technique Categories**

- Advanced: Sophisticated analytical concepts
- Implementation: Practical application during writing process
- Focus on how-to rather than pure theory
- Provide actionable guidance for writers

### **Key Concepts Section**

- 4 central concepts from this critical perspective
- Each gets: Definition + Application + Example Questions
- Make theory accessible to practicing writers
- Connect concepts to practical storytelling decisions

### **Final Reminder Paragraph**

- Always start with "\*Remember:"
- Summarize what this perspective reveals
- Connect to practical writing application
- End with ultimate goal for stories using this lens

## Suggested Critical Perspectives to Generate

### **Major Critical Schools**

- **Formalist Criticism** - Focus on text structure, language, literary devices
- **Feminist Criticism** - Gender dynamics, patriarchy, women's experiences
- **Marxist Criticism** - Class struggle, economic systems, power structures
- **Psychoanalytic Criticism** - Unconscious desires, psychological development
- **Postcolonial Criticism** - Colonial impact, cultural identity, power imbalances
- **New Historicism** - Historical context, cultural forces, power relationships

### **Contemporary Approaches**

- **Queer Theory** - Sexual identity, heteronormativity, gender performance
- **Critical Race Theory** - Racial dynamics, systemic racism, representation
- **Ecocriticism** - Environmental relationships, nature in literature
- **Disability Studies** - Representation of disability, ableism, accessibility
- **Reader-Response Theory** - Reader interpretation, subjective meaning-making
- **Deconstruction** - Language instability, meaning multiplicity, binary oppositions
